Blender iPad outline shader check (not a Blender build)

Keep the Blender engine's tabs closed. Do not clear Safari website data.

Extract this small ZIP. Move ipad-shader-check.html into the existing Blender
folder that WorldWideWeb serves (the folder containing index.html).
Do not replace any existing engine files or change the selected server folder.

Keep WorldWideWeb running and open Safari at:
http://localhost:8080/ipad-shader-check.html

Tap Check outline shader once. When finished, use Copy report or Download report
and send the report in the chat. If the whole iPad slows down, close the tab.

The page is self-contained and makes no engine requests. It creates one graphics
device, compiles four small shader variants and destroys the device. It does
not start Blender, access projects, register a worker or allocate scene textures.
Its 30-second deadline does not automatically retry.

The original fragment shader was captured from the exact delivered engine:
source commit 893f6163652f5cde223f9781a61ef4931f4b3444
artifact https://github.com/tntisthebomb/BlenderIpad/actions/runs/38705147615/artifacts/11958485469
original shader SHA256 90ae17e1cdfbd88290bf1a7839ba5fc30dd4889ebb09bb9f6439efa414418dd2

The other cases isolate unused sampler references and integer-load helper calls.
Their successful compilation in Chromium and matching outline pixels do not
establish that they compile on Safari. No full port completion is claimed.
